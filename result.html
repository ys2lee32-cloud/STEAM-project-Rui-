Experiment Results

We recorded temperature measurements every 5 minutes for 20 minutes. The data shows which insulation materials were most effective at preventing heat loss.

Material Start 5min 10min 15min 20min
Control 60.3 53.8 48.9 45.8 43.7
Cloth 62.1 59.7 57.1 56.4 54.2
Aluminium 60.5 57.3 55.1 53.5 53.0
Cotton 61.2 57.3 56.1 55.5 54.2
Rubberband 60.3 58.8 56.1 53.8 52.3
Bubblewrap 60.3 56.8 55.8 55.1 54.2
Average 60.8 57.4 54.9 53.4 51.9
Findings

Best Insulator: Bubble wrap showed the slowest rate of cooling

Second Best: Cotton

Least Effective: Aluminium foil (conducted heat away quickly)
WWW (What Went Well)
  • All team members participated actively
  • Measurements were recorded accurately

EBI (Even Better If)
  • We could have tested more materials
  • More trials would improve reliability
  • different starting point
Conclusion

Materials that trap air (like bubble wrap and cotton) are better insulators than materials that conduct heat (like aluminium foil). This experiment helps us understand why insulation is important in real-world applications like keeping homes warm.
