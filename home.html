Project Overview

This project investigates how different insulation materials affect the rate of heat loss from a container of hot water.
Materials Required
  • 5-6 Beakers
  • Bubble wrap
  • Aluminium foil
  • Cotton
  • Rubber band
  • Cloth
Hypothesis

If the container is insulated with materials that trap air effectively, then the rate of cooling will be slower.
