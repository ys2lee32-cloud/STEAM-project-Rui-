Experiment Procedure
  1. Fill 6 beakers with the same amount of hot water (200ml at 80°C)
  2. Measure and record the initial temperature of water in each beaker
  3. Wrap each beaker with a different insulation material:
    • Beaker 1: No insulation (control)
    • Beaker 2: Bubble wrap
    • Beaker 3: Aluminium foil
    • Beaker 4: Cotton
    • Beaker 5: Cloth
    • Beaker 6: Rubber band
  4. Record the temperature every 5 minutes for 30 minutes
  5. Calculate the rate of cooling for each material
  6. Compare the results to determine which material provides best insulation
Safety Precautions
  • Handle hot water carefully to avoid burns
  • Use heat-resistant gloves when necessary
  • Work in a well-ventilated area
  • Do not leave experiment unattended
Variables

Independent Variable: Type of insulation material

Dependent Variable: Rate of temperature decrease

Controlled Variables: Amount of water, initial temperature, room temperature, beaker size
